### 25-Word Pitch
PAISA brings clarity to your salary. Experience real-time daily budget reality checks, Indian bank statement imports, integer-exact paise calculations, and privacy-first local storage with zero banking passwords.

### 50-Word Description
PAISA is India's calm personal finance manager that translates your monthly salary into an intuitive daily spending allowance. With real-time daily reality checks, bank statement imports (CSV/JSON), integer-exact paise arithmetic, and offline-first encryption, PAISA helps you master your daily cash flow without ever requesting your banking passwords or reading your SMS.

### 150-Word Overview
Traditional budgeting tools fail because they give you a post-mortem report at the end of the month when your money is already gone. In India, where everyday spending happens in frictionless micro-bursts over UPI, you need clarity in the moment.

PAISA solves this by translating your monthly take-home salary into an intuitive daily spending allowance. Every grocery order, coffee run, and bill payment recalculates your daily buffer in real time.

Engineered specifically for Indian salary earners, PAISA features:
- **Instant Daily Reality Checks:** Know exactly what is safe to spend today.
- **Statement Imports:** Ingest statements from SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis without sharing banking credentials.
- **Integer Paise Engine:** Exact financial calculations with zero rounding errors.
- **100% Privacy-First:** Data stays inside your browser's encrypted vault by default.
