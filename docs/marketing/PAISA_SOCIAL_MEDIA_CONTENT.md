> We spend our entire careers working for our salaries, but modern payments have made money completely invisible.
>
> In India, UPI processed over 14 billion transactions last month. Micro-spending friction has dropped to zero. But personal budgeting tools haven't evolved: they either require giving away your NetBanking password, or they expect you to balance a 50-row spreadsheet every Sunday.
>
> That's why we built **PAISA** (https://paisa.money) — a calm, privacy-first daily money reality check designed specifically for Indian salary earners.
>
> Key principles:
> 1. Translates monthly salaries into intuitive daily spending allowances.
> 2. Zero banking credentials collected — ever.
> 3. Statement imports (CSV/JSON) with in-browser parsing.
> 4. Integer paise calculation engine (no floating-point rounding bugs).
> 5. 100% free core.
>
> Try the interactive demo and let me know your thoughts.

### LinkedIn Post 2: Engineering Integer Paise Math
> Why financial software should never use standard JavaScript floating-point numbers (`0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004`).
>
> When designing PAISA, we made an architectural decision: all calculations, from daily allowances to multi-year compound savings goals, operate in integer paise. ₹100.50 is stored as `10050`.
>
> This guarantees mathematical consistency across all calculations. Precision isn't a feature; it's a financial obligation.

### LinkedIn Post 3: The Privacy Trade-Off in Indian Fintech
> Why do personal finance apps in India insist on reading your entire SMS inbox?
>
> Convenience shouldn't require surrendering your personal communications. PAISA takes the opposite path: non-custodial, client-side, zero SMS permissions. You stay in full control.

### LinkedIn Post 4: The Problem with Monthly Budgeting
> If you give someone ₹60,000 on the 1st of the month, their brain perceives abundance. By the 22nd, panic sets in.
>
> By dividing disposable income across remaining calendar days, PAISA grounds you in real-time. A ₹400 coffee isn't "part of a ₹60,000 pool"; it's 25% of today's ₹1,600 allowance.

### LinkedIn Post 5: The Journey from Concept to Production
> 8 development levels, 134 automated tests, and hundreds of hours refining the experience. PAISA is now production-ready. Built with care for every Indian salary earner.
