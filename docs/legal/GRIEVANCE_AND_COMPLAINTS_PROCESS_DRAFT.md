## 1. COMMITMENT TO FAIR AND PROMPT REDRESSAL

PAISA is committed to resolving user complaints, service defects, and privacy concerns in an open, prompt, and accountable manner.

---

## 2. HOW TO LODGE A GRIEVANCE

Users may submit complaints through either of the following channels:

### Channel A: In-App Feedback & Support
Navigate to **Settings $\to$ Help & Support** or use the **"Send Feedback / Report a Bug"** dialog on any public page.

### Channel B: Email to the Data Protection & Grievance Officer
- **Email:** [GRIEVANCE EMAIL ADDRESS, e.g., grievance@paisa.money]
- **Subject Line:** *Grievance / DPDP Inquiry — [Your Name / Account Email]*
- **Required Details:**
  1. Your registered email address.
  2. Description of the issue or privacy concern.
  3. Relevant screenshots or error codes (without sharing sensitive personal documents).

---

## 3. STATUTORY RESOLUTION ESCALATION TIMELINES

| Stage | Action | Mandatory Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1: Acknowledgment** | Automated ticket generation & human confirmation of receipt. | Within **48 hours** |
| **Level 2: Investigation & Action** | Technical review, log inspection, or corrective data adjustment. | Within **15 calendar days** |
| **Level 3: Final Resolution** | Comprehensive written reply and grievance closure. | Within **30 calendar days** |

---

## 4. APPELLATE REMEDIES UNDER INDIAN LAW

If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided by our Grievance Officer within 30 days:
- You have the right to register a complaint with the **Data Protection Board of India (DPBI)** in accordance with Section 28 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
- For consumer service disputes, you may approach the **National Consumer Helpline (NCH)** via `consumerhelpline.gov.in` or toll-free at `1915`.
- Cybersecurity incident reports can also be submitted to **CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)** at `incident@cert-in.org.in`.
