## 2. PROVISIONS FOR FUTURE OPTIONAL PAID PLANS (PAISA PRO)

If PAISA introduces optional premium convenience tiers (such as multi-device cloud backups, automated bank feed adapters, or advanced team sharing) in the future:

### 2.1 Subscription Cancellation
- Users may cancel recurring subscriptions at any time with a single click via **Settings $\to$ Subscription Management**.
- Upon cancellation, you will retain access to premium features until the end of the current billing cycle. No further automatic recurring charges will occur.

### 2.2 Refund Window (14-Day Money-Back Guarantee)
- If you are unsatisfied with a paid tier for any reason, you may request a 100% refund within **14 calendar days** of the initial charge.
- Refund requests submitted after 14 days will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis (e.g. verified technical failure).

### 2.3 Refund Processing Timeline
- Approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment source (UPI account, debit card, credit card, or net banking) via our RBI-authorized payment gateway partner within **5 to 7 business days**.
