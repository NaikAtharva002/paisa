## 1. THE FOUNDATIONAL PHILOSOPHY: TRUST BEFORE REVENUE

Many Indian fintech apps monetize aggressively by selling loan leads, insurance policies, or high-risk trading products to their users. This creates a severe conflict of interest: the app benefits when the user is financially strained or takes on high-interest debt.

**PAISA rejects predatory monetization.** We align directly with user prosperity:
- **Core personal finance is free:** Anyone can track expenses, manage budgets, and calculate daily allowances without paying a single Rupee.
- **No loan spam:** We will never send SMS or WhatsApp messages encouraging users to take instant personal loans.
- **Value-based convenience pricing:** Future revenue will come strictly from optional software convenience features (PAISA Pro), not data selling or loan commissions.

## 3. UNIT ECONOMICS & PRICING RATIONALE

- **Why ₹99/month?** In India, digital subscriptions priced under ₹100/month (like YouTube Premium Individual at ₹129 or Spotify at ₹119) experience low churn and high spontaneous conversion among young working professionals.
- **Annual Discount:** ₹899/year (a 25% discount) locks in recurring annual cash flow while remaining accessible.
- **Gross Margins:** Because PAISA processes data locally on client devices, server compute costs per user are exceptionally low (< ₹3 / active user / month), resulting in projected software gross margins of **95%+**.
